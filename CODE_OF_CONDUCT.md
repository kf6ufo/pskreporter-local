# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give constructive feedback and accept it graciously.
- Focus discussion on the project and what is best for its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal attacks, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. If no private method is available, open an issue requesting private contact, but do not include sensitive incident details. Maintainers will protect reporters' privacy as far as reasonably possible and review reports fairly.

Behavior that violates GitHub's policies may also be reported directly to GitHub.
